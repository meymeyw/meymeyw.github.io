Common Ground is a lightweight facilitation toolkit for community groups who need to gather input and make decisions together.

## The challenge

The project began with workshops where people described meetings that were hard to join, hard to follow, or hard to remember afterward.

## What I did

- Mapped the workshop journey and identified points where people lost context.
- Prototyped a shared agenda and a simple decision log.
- Worked with participants to make the language clear and welcoming.
