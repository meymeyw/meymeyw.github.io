Field Notes is a small digital garden for saving fleeting observations: a half-heard sentence, a color combination, or a place worth returning to.

## The idea

I wanted a calm, low-pressure place to collect fragments without turning them into a feed. The interface is intentionally simple and works just as well on a phone as on a laptop.

## What I did

- Designed the visual language and responsive layouts.
- Built the searchable, tag-based archive.
- Tested the writing flow with a small group of friends.

## Reflection

The best part was learning how little structure a personal tool needs. A good empty page can be an invitation.
